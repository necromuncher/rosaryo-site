The Rosary, one step at a time.

Rosaryo opens to today’s mysteries and guides you from the first Sign of the Cross to the last Amen. Tap to count each Hail Mary, and go back whenever you lose your place.

  1. Begin with the Creed

    The Sign of the Cross, the Apostles’ Creed, the Our Father and three Hail Marys.

  2. Pray five decades

    Each decade names its mystery, then the Our Father, ten Hail Marys with a tap counter, the Glory Be and the Fatima Prayer.

  3. Close with the Hail, Holy Queen

    Finish with the Hail, Holy Queen and the Sign of the Cross.

Mysteries for every day
Joyful on Monday and Saturday, Luminous on Thursday, Sorrowful on Tuesday and Friday, Glorious on Wednesday and Sunday.
English and Filipino
Switch the prayers, mysteries and labels between English and Filipino at any time, even in the middle of a decade.
Word of the Lord
A different Bible passage for every day of the year, delivered at the time you choose. Turn it off in Settings whenever you like.
Private by design
No account, no ads and no tracking. Your settings stay on your phone, and the prayers work without an internet connection.
