Privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Rosaryo does not collect, share or sell any personal information. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

Who we are

Rosaryo is an Android app made by Erwin Alegro in Lucena City, Quezon, Philippines. In this policy, “we” and “the app” mean Rosaryo and its developer.

Information we collect

None. Rosaryo has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any device identifier. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking code, and it does not send information from your phone to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

The app saves three settings in its private storage on your device: your language (English or Filipino), whether the daily Word of the Lord notification is on, and the time you chose for it. These settings are never sent anywhere. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

The prayers, mysteries and Bible passages are included in the app, so it works without an internet connection.

Permissions the app asks for

Notifications
On Android 13 and newer, Rosaryo asks before it shows the daily Word of the Lord. You can turn it off in the app’s Settings or in Android settings at any time.
Alarms and reminders
Lets Android deliver the notification at the time you chose.
Run at startup
Lets the app schedule your daily reminders again after your phone restarts.

These permissions are used only for the daily reminder.

Third parties

Rosaryo includes no third-party advertising or analytics services. It is built with the open-source Ionic and Capacitor frameworks, which run on your device and do not collect data about you. If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and installation under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from Google Play.

Children

Rosaryo can be used by people of any age. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your choices

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. To remove the settings stored on your phone, clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

This website

This website is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operation of the service. We do not use cookies or analytics on this website. Its pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when you visit.

Changes to this policy

If the app or this policy changes, we will post the new version here with an updated effective date. If we ever begin collecting personal information, we will say so here and in the app before we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to erwin.alegro@gmail.com.
